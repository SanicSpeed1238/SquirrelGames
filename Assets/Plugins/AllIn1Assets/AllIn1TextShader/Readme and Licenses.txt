## Platform Requirements

The text effects read per character data from compute buffers in the shader, so they need a GPU that supports compute buffers in vertex shaders:
- Supported: DirectX 11 and 12, Metal, Vulkan, desktop OpenGL 4.3 or later, and OpenGL ES 3.1 devices whose GPU exposes buffers to vertex shaders.
- Not supported: WebGL, OpenGL ES 3.0, and OpenGL ES 3.1 GPUs without vertex shader buffers (common on older Android devices).

On unsupported platforms the text still renders with its face color and dilate, but without shader effects. The animations (typewriter, tags, spline, warp) keep working.
